Push Guidance Sweep

Noise-optimisation guidance for block pushing, scored by the simulated cube-centroid distance to target after SAPIEN replay of the best imagined plan. Population 3, 60 transitions, full churn, t_freeze 0.8 unless stated.

1

The scene builder was re-posing clouds but not body_templates. Every predicted frame rebuilt the cube at the template episode's yaw (66.85°), so the block snapped a median 25° on frame 1 with the rod 44 mm away. Fixed; the snap is now 0.0°.

2

t_freeze is the one lever that moved the simulated result. Optimising bank draws down to noise level 0.5 instead of 0.8 (540 of 1200 draws instead of 207): 51.7 vs 58.1 mm on all 50 scenarios, paired +6.4 mm, wins 29–20. t_freeze 0.3 and 0.65 land between, at 56.5 and 61.4 on the 15.

3

Step size, iterations and schedule barely move the simulated result. lr 0.05 → 0.3 at S=6 spans 59.5–64.9 mm on the 15; 6 vs 12 iterations 58.1 vs 57.7 on 50; decay vs constant 57.8 vs 58.1. Imagination improves monotonically with step size; the sim gap grows with it.

4

The optimizer is not descending. Consecutive iterates swing ~20 mm in imagination and the best plan sits at iteration 0–2 in 20 of 50 cases. Best-of-N sampling, not gradient descent.

5

S=10, shell-off and Adam are all worse. S=10: 66.7–82.3 mm. Shell-off: 66.0–68.6. Adam with shell: 77.5.

6

Generation is not reproducible at a fixed seed. Three identical unguided requests differed by 1.6–79 mm per point; two runs of identical guided settings differed by 19.6 mm paired std (max 57) on 15 scenarios. Arm differences under ~10 mm on 15 scenarios are inside this noise. Cause not yet diagnosed.

7

Guidance works. Unguided ends ~85 mm from target; the best arm ends 51.7 mm. The sim/imagination gap (30–36 mm) is what caps it.
