Lift Over Wall

Pickup-only checkpoint, jar (val batch000_e1). Newest: goal +15 cm x +15 cm z over a 10 cm wall at +7.5 cm, 60 frames, 20 it. Also: goal +25 cm x +15 cm z (wall at +12.5 cm, 10 or 13 cm, or none; 60 frames, 10 or 20 it) and goal +15/+15 without a wall (39 frames, 10 it, shell on/off). Wall = grey box 2 cm thick, 30 cm wide. S=10, Adam lr 0.1, population 4. Isaac floating-hand replay overlaid (toggle 'simulated'). Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, shift-drag to pan. Pick a domain, a run (newest first), then a member; layers toggle below the viewer.

Method. Streaming rollout of N transitions from frame 0, every action hidden. Full churn (η = 1): each active row is re-formed from the model's x0 with a pre-decided draw x = x0 + l (E[row, i] − x0); the rollout is a deterministic function of the draw bank E. Guidance: J = ‖c_obj(x_N) − target‖ + w_box · clearance shortfall + w_floor · depth, gradient through every model call (activation-checkpointed), update of the free draws (level ≥ t_freeze) by Adam or a unit-gradient step, best (member, iteration) kept; iteration 0 = unguided. Simulated layer = the generated actuator trajectory played back in the domain's simulator (Isaac floating hand / Genesis MPM / SAPIEN PushBoundary), the object left to physics.
