Closed vs Open Loop Pile Manipulation

Cloud-centroid scenarios 0 to 4 of the paper's seed-42 set: move the dough centroid to a target 7.5 to 15 cm away in 150 frames. Same checkpoint (pwm_v1_mould_ctxgen_100m_uncond_unet_bidir/step_60000.pt), same sampler (stream staircase, 19-row windows on every model call, tail padded, full churn on pose and particle rows), same guidance (3 members, S 6, unitgrad lr 0.1, 6 iterations, shell, t_freeze 0.8, fixed FPS, seed 0). Open loop: one 150-frame plan, all executed. Closed loop: plan, execute 25 frames in Genesis (re-simulated from the start), feed back the last 3 executed transitions as clean context rows, replan the remaining frames (at least 16), reward always on task frame 150. The closed loop's first plan equals the open-loop plan. Distances are dough centroid to target in the plane after Genesis MPM replay.

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan. Every 2nd frame, every 2nd dough particle (512 of 1024) and every 2nd paddle point are drawn. Red sphere = target (dough centroid, in-plane). Runs: /data/user_data/mbronars/packages/guided_playback/runs/mould/olmould_s42_ep00* and clmould_s42_ep00*_k25 (per-replan plans in *_r0 .. *_r5, Genesis executions in exec_r*). Driver: bin/closed_loop_mould.py; sampler: gp/adapters/paper_prefix.py; page data: bin/build_mould_cl_data.py.
